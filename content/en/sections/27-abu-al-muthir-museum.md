This museum sits above Sabah Abi Al Mu'thir, together with part of the Al Aqur Wall. A visitor to it can try walking on the Al Aqur Wall and see the room that was used for the watch, and climb one of the towers. He will also learn important historical information and the old methods of guarding and defence.
