title: The water drainage channels
order: 20
---

Since ancient times Omani engineering has taken account, in its building, of draining rainwater through the quarters and between the houses in the lanes. So do not be surprised to find these openings spread throughout the quarter. You may see rainwater running through them, passing beneath the houses, until it empties into the channels of the *aflaj* and the farms.
