The older people of Nizwa still remember the hospital of the American doctor Thoms — not only because they were treated there, but because they preserve the memory of his treating the Imam of Oman, **Muhammad bin Abdullah Al Khalili**, when blindness came upon his eyes and God healed him through this doctor's care.

They also tell many stories and reports of him that they witnessed themselves, arising from his deep admiration for the Imam's character. When he returned to his own country he spoke to the clergy in America of his admiration for the Imam, saying that the Imam represented the character of the Prophet Abraham. Sheikh Al Khalili related this himself.
