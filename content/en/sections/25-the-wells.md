No old Omani house is without a well of its own, used for drinking, bathing, cooking and washing. These wells differ from farm wells in the narrowness of their bore: no more than about one metre on average, whereas the bore of a farm well sometimes reaches three or four metres, at a depth of up to twenty metres — less or more according to the water available in that place.

The water of the wells and the *aflaj* is notable for being warm in winter and cool in summer.
