Construction of Nizwa Castle began in the second century AH, some twelve hundred years ago, in the time of Imam **Muhammad bin Abdullah bin Abi Affan**. Additions followed in the time of Imam **Al Salt bin Malik Al Kharusi** and of the Imams who came after him. The largest additions were probably made under Imam Al Salt bin Malik, coinciding with Oman's strength on land and at sea and with the great fleet the Imam sent to the relief of the people of **Socotra**, oppressed by the Christians, after a woman named Fatima Al Suqutriya appealed to him in a famous ode setting out what they had done.

With that same fleet the Imam also cleared the Indian Ocean of pirates, after the emperor of India complained to him of their attacks on merchants and their plundering of shipping — for which the emperor presented Imam Al Salt with a precious Indian sword.

The castle is counted among the grand Omani fortifications. It contains rooms, wells, stores, prisons, rooms for teaching and for provisions, and other chambers besides. Nizwa Fort was later built directly alongside it.
