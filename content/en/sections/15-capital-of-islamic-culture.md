Nizwa was chosen as **Capital of Islamic Culture for the year 2015**, for its great standing in learning and for its many contributions to religion across Omani history. How could it not be — it is the seedbed of Islam, the throne of the Arabs, the seat of the Imamate, and the city of learning and of scholars. It is among the most productive Arab cities in scholarship, and it holds an advanced place in the number of its scholars and the volume of its books and its writing.

There were periods in Nizwa's history when not one of its houses was without a scholar in it; a merchant, a porter or a builder might be enough to answer your question on a point of learning, so many were the scholars in it.

For that reason it was celebrated in **2015** as Capital of Islamic Culture, and this commemorative tower was raised for it on the frontage of the souq as a witness to it.
