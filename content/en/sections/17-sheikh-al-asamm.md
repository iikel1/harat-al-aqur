title: The grave of Sheikh Al Asamm

The grave of the learned Sheikh Al Asamm — Abu Abdullah Uthman bin Abdullah bin Ahmad Al Azri — lies behind Al Shawadhna Mosque and west of Al Jalilain School. He was among the leading scholars of the seventh century AH.

Wilayat Nizwa holds the graves of many Imams and scholars, but what is striking about this Sheikh's grave is that it sits in the middle of the quarter — most scholars' and Imams' graves being in the well-known cemetery west of the wilayat, and some on the edges of the villages.

This Sheikh was not in fact deaf, as his byname suggests. There is a charming story behind it, known to the people here: a woman came to him to ask a ruling, and in the course of her question a sound of wind escaped her without her meaning it. The Sheikh, to spare her the embarrassment, at once asked her to repeat her question — feigning inattention, with his hand at his ear, as though his hearing were weak.
