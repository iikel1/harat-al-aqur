Omanis were accustomed to roasting some of the meat of their slaughtered animals by burying it in ovens fired with the wood of natural trees, on the days of Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha, in a fine social setting: the people of each quarter gather to make the Omani *shuwa*. It is a social custom still alive today.

For some of these ovens the participants pay nothing at all, because they have endowments of their own; the people take part in the work instead, and enjoy themselves in the doing.
