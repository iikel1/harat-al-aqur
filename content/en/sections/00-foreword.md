In the name of God. Praise be to God, who possesses all favour, all giving, all generosity and all bounty; the honour and the blessing are His, who gives and so ennobles, who grants and so creates anew.

Harat Al Aqur in Nizwa is still shaking off the dust of neglect and forgetting, and putting on instead the sash of revival and attention, so that the houses of its people may be lived in again after restoration — beautiful in their engineering and their fine design. The owners of its houses are still repairing and restoring their homes, keeping faith with what their forefathers made. From the moment Bawariq Nizwa Heritage Company began the first restoration the quarter had ever seen, the effort has been sustained by every party to this national duty.

To complete that work, and to keep pace with the quarter's development, this book was made to serve an important side of the tourism and cultural field, supported with photographs to add to its beauty and its clarity.

After this modest effort: whatever I have done well in it is from God, and where I have fallen short, this is the limit of my ability and my knowledge. Praise be to God, Lord of the Worlds.

**Sulaiman bin Muhammad Al Sulaimani** Wilayat Nizwa — Al Aqur village
