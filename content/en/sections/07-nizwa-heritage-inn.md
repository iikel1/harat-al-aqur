Nizwa Heritage Inn was opened under the patronage of His Excellency Sheikh Hamad bin Salim bin Saif Al Aghbari, Wali of Nizwa, on **1 Dhu al-Qa'da 1439 AH, corresponding to 23 April 2018**.

The company had undertaken this initiative from its founding, to turn the quarter into a tourist destination, to preserve the heritage of the forefathers, to show later generations how their ancestors lived, to encourage residents and house-owners to make good use of the place, and to carry on the work of restoring every landmark in the quarter — houses, schools, *sablahs*, towers and walls. That is the goal being sought.

This site holds a large place in the affections of the older generation, who used to meet here to look over the news, to read, and to take coffee. That attachment shows in the number of older people who come to it, and who then tell the stories and the anecdotes of the quarter while they enjoy their coffee — living, for a while, as though they were in the past.
