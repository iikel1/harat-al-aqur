Across most Omani quarters — as is still the case today — there were many schools for the Qur'an, for the sciences of the language and of literature, and for some other subjects. In Harat Al Aqur there is a group of such schools; parts of some of their walls survive, and some have recently been restored. Endowments were assigned to them long ago.

Out of these small schools came a great many major scholars, who afterwards became men of state with real standing. The size of the school never mattered as much as the learning and the teacher.
