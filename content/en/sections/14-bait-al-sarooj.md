title: Bait Al Sarooj

---

One of the largest houses in Harat Al Aqur, and among the grandest and most solid. Although many of the houses in this quarter were built with *sarooj*, this house alone became known by the name.

It was formerly lived in by a number of important political figures, among them **Sayyid Tariq bin Taimur Al Busaidi**, father of Sultan Haitham bin Tariq — may God protect him.
