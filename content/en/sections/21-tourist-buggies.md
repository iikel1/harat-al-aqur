Bawariq Nizwa International Investment Company, represented by Nizwa Heritage Inn, signed an agreement with one of the Omani companies to operate **electric vehicles for tourist tours** — making it the first operator of such vehicles in the Sultanate, in continuation of the quarter's development.

Any visitor can take a sightseeing and orientation tour in them; they are now to be found throughout Harat Al Aqur across the winter season.
