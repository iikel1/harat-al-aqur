title: The Persians' graveyard

This burial ground lies in a lane known as Sikkat Al Qabr — the lane of the grave — named for a grave at its entrance. It is a mass grave for a large number of Persians.

They entered Oman in the last days of the Ya'ariba state, and entered Nizwa on **1 Dhu al-Hijja 1150 AH**, and worked corruption in it. Their number was large — it is said **four thousand** soldiers — and they came upon the town while its people were unaware. They killed children and old men, seized women and violated what should have been inviolable. The people therefore set an ambush for them, drawing them into the quarter and shutting its gates so that they could not escape, and most of them were killed. The country was spared their evil, and a great number of them were buried in this pit in one large communal grave.

That has been the way of Omanis with whoever invaded them and took their land as lawful plunder: no settlement until he is finished with and security restored to the country. It is why some have called Oman the graveyard of invaders.
