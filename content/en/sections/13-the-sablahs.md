Harat Al Aqur is divided internally into five sub-quarters, and not one of them is without a *majlis* or *sablah* where the residents gather regularly. There they exchange their news, honour their guests and share their food — for their life was a social one, and their neighbourliness was brotherly, bound by religion and by good conduct towards those next door.
