Nizwa Souq is at its best on the days of the two Eids, with popular celebrations, stalls selling children's toys, and every kind of fast food made in the houses that you may not find on any other day of the year.

And because Nizwa is a school for merchants, you will enjoy dealing with hundreds of traders under the age of eighteen — most of the sellers being Omanis, and some of them perhaps ten or eight years old. Even so you will find in them boldness, courage, enthusiasm and real experience in dealing.

This is an old custom on this occasion, and most families come to it, bringing their sons and their children with them. The children keep the time of the Eid from their earliest years and would not trade it for anything else — delighted with the money they have made from their Eid gift.
