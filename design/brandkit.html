02 · CONSTRUCTION

Where the mark comes from

The wall, reduced

Not a drawn symbol. This is the quarter's own circuit, traced from Dr Al Salmi's plan and cut to 25 vertices. The four gaps are its four sabah — the wall's only openings — so the way in is negative space.

The four gates

صباح السوق صباح الشجبي صباح الصبخة صباح أبي المؤثر

Below 16 px the gate gaps close and the mark reads as a plain ring. That is the floor — do not set it smaller.

Two things this sheet does not settle. The mark comes from a plan traced out of a 753 px book illustration — close enough to identify the quarter, but it is not a survey and carries no coordinates. And the photograph is the book's, so it stands here as direction only: rights to the book's text and images are still an open conversation with the author.
